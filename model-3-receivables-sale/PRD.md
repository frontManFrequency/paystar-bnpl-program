## 1. Product boundary and source traceability

The product is an Iranian, Persian-first, right-to-left BNPL service for adult customers of contracted clinics. Dental consultation and treatment are the initial categories. A clinic is a `business` tenant and can both refer customers and sell services. Existing merchant/store behavior is retained as a general service/catalog interface; an inviter and selling clinic may differ. A referral never grants the inviter ownership of another clinic's receivable.

PayStar supports acquisition, registration, identity and account verification, assessment, application review, credit limits, clinic checkout, four installments, checks, settlements, refunds, reminders, collections, reporting and customer support. No clinical diagnoses, medical records, prescriptions or treatment recommendations are collected. Treatment delivery and quality belong to the clinic. A customer can raise a service dispute, but only the selling clinic can approve a financial return.

Changes from the original are deliberate: the bank is final customer approver only in Model 4; a clinic is final approver in Model 2. Loan payment always settles to the current creditor, even when initiated in PayStar. Late payment is `OVERDUE` from day 1 and operational `DEFAULT` at day 30, separating missed due date from legal enforcement. Penalties default to zero. A failed/undelivered assessment or duplicate fee charge is refunded; an executed assessment remains non-refundable after disclosure and required review. These replace unsafe ambiguities rather than being hidden exceptions.

## 3. Registration, evidence and review requirements

1. The clinic creates a revocable, signed referral URL with business ID, model ID and opaque referral token. Token lasts 30 days. Opening it displays business name, legal creditor, four-payment example, assessment fee and privacy terms. The first valid attribution is locked when application is submitted; changing clinic requires a new draft and explicit customer consent.
2. Customer enters national ID, mobile and birth date, verifies a 6-digit SMS OTP, and accepts independent identity, account and credit-report consents. OTP expires in 2 minutes, resend after 60 seconds, 5 attempts per challenge, 5 sends per hour per mobile and IP; excess causes a 30-minute throttle. Consent is not prechecked; marketing is optional and separately revocable.
3. Collect name, family name, father's name, ID-book number, national ID, gender as source-provided/unknown, birth date, birthplace, mobile, optional landline, current address and postal code. Provider-verified fields are read-only; mismatch enters correction/manual evidence review, never silent overwrite. Missing optional landline does not block submission.
4. Customer enters card number through the account-verification integration. Resolve IBAN, account number, bank name and account-holder identity; national-ID match is mandatory. The browser/backend discard full PAN after tokenization; store provider token, bank identifiers and last four digits. Never collect CVV2, card PIN or banking passwords.
5. Upload national-ID evidence and income proof as PDF/JPG/PNG, maximum 10 MB each, maximum 10 files per application. Scan malware before review, quarantine unsafe files. Raw file access is purpose-scoped and logged. Prototype identity values are synthetic and never submitted to a real provider.
6. Fee confirmation must precede a credit-report request. Provider timeout enters `EXTERNAL_PENDING`, with status and retry time visible. PayStar reviews completeness, score band, income calculation, duplicate/fraud signals and rationale. A correction lists exact field paths and document categories, not a vague rejection.
7. Final reviewer follows model authority. Approval sets limit, expiry, policy snapshot and inviter-only flag (default false). Only paystar-admin may set that flag before signing; after signing any restriction change needs a new consented agreement and cannot invalidate existing purchases. Model 2 additionally always scopes the line to its creditor clinic, independent of this flag.
8. Customer sees live status, reason summary, current owner and expected next action. Internal fraud details are withheld; customer gets a support/appeal path. Reapply creates a linked new application, preserving history. An appeal is reviewed by a different authorized reviewer within 2 business days.

## 4. Credit, checkout, contracts and checks

**Credit equation:** `available = max(0, limit - active_reservations - outstanding_principal)`; held or overdue lines expose available for explanation but `spendable = 0`. Outstanding principal counts unrepaid, uncancelled funded principal, including assigned principal in Model 3. A receivables sale, guarantee payment, write-off or facility draw does not recycle the customer's credit. Only customer principal repayments, approved principal cancellation or a legally effective debt discharge do so.

At checkout, clinic submits signed order reference, customer, service line items, gross service principal P and selected line. Backend reprices from authoritative clinic quote and rejects missing inventory, stale price, ineligible clinic, changed amount, expired approval, unpaid overdue debt and insufficient principal capacity. Simulate the new schedule together with all existing active schedules and external monthly obligations: in every affected Jalali month, total required payments must be <=30% of verified net monthly income; reject AFFORDABILITY_EXCEEDED otherwise, even when nominal line capacity remains. Reserve the whole P, not just the final three installments, under a transaction locking customer and clinic/program exposure. The customer must have enough available credit for P. Reserve treasury funding separately for funded models.

Present a frozen quote with P, four principal shares, commission, due dates, total, current legal creditor, check beneficiary, refund terms, fee, service location, policy version and signed hash. Customer signs the relevant framework/per-purchase agreement and three postdated checks, one for each installment after the first. Check face = its scheduled principal + commission, no blank or unlimited check. Metadata includes Sayad ID, issuer identity, beneficiary national/legal ID, amount, maturity, verified registration and acceptance evidence, custody holder and scan hash. A check status in the application is never proof of external registration. Bank-authorized manual evidence with maker/checker may be used where there is no licensed API.

For Models 1/3, the initial PayStar agreement may allow a separately approved substitute customer guarantee (bank guarantee, specified cash collateral) instead of checks. Default is three checks; substitute instrument must have equal covered unpaid face, named beneficiary, expiry beyond final maturity by 90 days, verified enforceability and signed policy. Models 2/4 baseline require checks. No security is drawn before a verified debt and authorized recovery instruction.

After contract and security readiness, the first installment is collected into the model's creditor route. Verified provider confirmation atomically commits purchase and schedule, consumes reservation and records first installment payment. It releases first principal only once. Final customer purchase status is `CONFIRMED`; service remains `AWAITING_FUNDS` until funded-model clinic payout is verified, then `READY_FOR_SERVICE`. Model 2 becomes ready after first installment is received. Clinic records service start and completion with customer acknowledgement. A missing acknowledgment opens a dispute; it does not prove delivery by itself.

A customer may choose less than the full service price for BNPL. The difference is a separate cash contribution paid directly to clinic and evidenced before checkout completion. P always denotes the financed service portion; cash contribution is not included in the credit line, four installments or commission. A return allocates service cancellation proportionately between financed P and cash contribution; clinic refunds the latter outside BNPL with a separate receipt.

## 5. Monetary formulas and worked examples

Let P be financed principal in rial, b=1200 bps. `C = floor((P*b + 5000)/10000)` uses half-up integer rounding. Split P and C independently: installments 1–3 take `floor(total/4)` and installment 4 takes the remainder. Each payable amount is `P_i + C_i + assessed_penalty_i`. This is a flat price addition, not 12% per month or an APR claim. The signed screen shows the complete price, not a misleading interest-rate equivalence.

**40 million toman example:** P=400,000,000 IRR; C=48,000,000 IRR; total=448,000,000 IRR; each principal=100,000,000 IRR and commission=12,000,000 IRR. Four payments of 112,000,000 IRR (11.2 million toman), first now, then 1/2/3 months later. First payment leaves 300,000,000 IRR principal outstanding and restores 100,000,000 IRR capacity on a 400,000,000 IRR line. Assessment fee 500,000 IRR is separate. Customer's total including that application fee is 448,500,000 IRR if this is the sole purchase on that assessment.

Early payment after installment 1 owes 336,000,000 IRR, not another 448,000,000 IRR. After installments 1 and 2 it owes 224,000,000 IRR. Commission already paid is never charged twice; unearned scheduled commission is not waived by early repayment in the baseline, subject to legal/bank release approval. Multiple selected installments are processed earliest due first. Within each installment allocate principal, then commission, then valid penalty. Overpayment is an unapplied customer liability, returned to verified account or wallet after reconciliation; it creates no extra credit.

Partial payments are supported for existing installments, but checkout's first installment must be paid in full before confirmation. An installment becomes `PAID` only when all non-waived components are zero. Credit release follows principal actually allocated even if commission remains overdue. A new overdue commission balance still blocks spending. Payment reversals remove the original allocations and principal release; if available capacity becomes negative internally, show 0, block spending and open a recovery case rather than dropping debt.

## 6. Settlement, cancellation, returns and withdrawal

Funded-model clinic payout is the entire financed P; first installment principal is part of the funding cash, not a second merchant payment. Model 2 clinic receives each actual P_i+C_i gradually; PayStar never promises an upfront advance. Funded-model settlement target is next business day after purchase confirmation and security verification, 14:00 processing cutoff. Customer schedule is unchanged by payout delay. At 2 business days unresolved, operations must obtain verified payout or process a full clinic-authorized cancellation before service begins. Failed/unknown transfer is reconciled before retry.

Before first payment: clinic or customer may abandon/cancel checkout, releasing reservations and reserved treasury capacity. If payment is externally pending, cancel the payment intent and wait for definitive provider result; late success is a refundable unapplied receipt. After first verified payment, any cancellation is a return with all required accounting entries, never deletion of purchase or schedule. Non-delivery complaints trigger support; only clinic can submit return approval, with operations mediating within 2 business days.

Clinic approves full or partial service-value return with order, line item, reason, amount and evidence. Completed medical services are not automatically refundable; the financial system follows the clinic's accepted monetary return. A full return refunds all paid principal and commission, cancels all unpaid principal and commission, and releases only principal still outstanding. Paid penalties are not refunded and unpaid valid penalties remain payable. Assessment fee is separate and unaffected except its own failed-service rules.

**Deterministic partial return:** let cumulative returned financed principal R be 0<=R<=P. For each original principal and commission component, calculate target cancellation/refund share by proportional allocation with largest-remainder rounding (ties by installment number). Total target principal=R; total target commission=round_half_up(C*R/P). For each component let O be its original amount, Paid its cumulative allocated payments net of payment reversals (without subtracting previous return refunds), and T its cumulative proportional return target. Set target_cancel=min(T, O-Paid), target_refund=T-target_cancel. Apply delta_cancel=target_cancel-prior_cancelled and delta_refund=target_refund-prior_refunded. Under valid component constraints both deltas are nonnegative; use original O-Paid, not the already-adjusted remaining balance. This prevents drift and negative adjustments when payments occur between multiple returns. Original amounts remain immutable. Outstanding principal cancelled releases credit once; already repaid principal refunded to wallet does not release credit again. Refund does not redraw a loan. A 50% return immediately after installment 1 of the example refunds 56,000,000 IRR from installment 1 and cancels 56,000,000 IRR in each later installment; remaining outstanding principal is 150,000,000 IRR. A full return at that point refunds 112,000,000 IRR and cancels 336,000,000 IRR.

Approval books a wallet liability for the paid refundable portion immediately and a claim against the accountable clinic/creditor. PayStar uses the prefunded bridge so the customer does not wait for partner recovery. Each wallet credit must have segregated cash backing, never a fictitious credit balance. If bridge funds are exhausted, book `REFUND_PAYABLE` immediately, clearly show amount owed and delayed availability, prioritize existing liabilities, pause originations and treasury replenishes within 1 business day; this is an incident, not a permission to deny a return. Commission refund is owed by its economic recipient; merchant refunds the relevant service principal. Model-specific owner compensation is defined below.

Wallet is a refund ledger, not a payment/credit product: no top-up, peer transfer or paying new purchases. Withdraw to the same customer's verified IBAN, minimum 10,000 IRR (final account closure can withdraw less), maximum 400,000,000 IRR per day, no withdrawal fee. Reserve balance before submitting payout. Target next business day, and status remains pending on ambiguous response. Failed definitive payout releases reservation; success consumes it once. Beneficiary changes require re-verification and 24-hour cooling period. KYC review may temporarily hold disbursement with reason and escalation; it never erases customer money.

## 7. Reminders, collections, guarantees and disputes

Send SMS and in-app reminders D-3, D-1 and D0 at 09:00 Tehran, then D+1, D+3 and D+7. On D+8 create a case assigned to an authorized collector; one human contact attempt per day maximum, between 09:00 and 18:00 Tehran. No diagnosis or debt detail in SMS beyond a secure account link and generic reminder. Stop reminders on settled/cancelled installment; suppress duplicates by installment, channel and template occurrence.

DPD is local calendar-date difference from earliest unpaid due date, with due-date cutoff applied. DPD30 marks operational default and packages debt statement, verified payments, contract, security chain and prior contact history for creditor approval. Enforcement requires creditor authorization, legal review evidence and security holder match. A guarantee claim reduces economic creditor loss but leaves customer debt outstanding until legal discharge/subrogation is recorded. Track collector, beneficiary, recovery source, allocation and acquired recovery rights so nobody collects the same balance twice.

Clinic support options are explicitly implemented as contract profiles: NONE (default), cash deposit, verified bank guarantee/instrument, real-estate collateral, percentage loss share, defaulted-receivable repurchase, or reserve withheld from settlements. Default demo guarantee profile for selected fixtures is 20% of outstanding defaulted principal after customer/check recovery attempts, capped at 500,000,000 IRR aggregate per clinic, no fees or penalties covered. Trigger is DPD60, after legal case opened and at least 15 calendar days of documented customer/check recovery; enforce only after creditor/legal maker-checker approval. Max claim=min(20% × residual defaulted principal, unused cap, enforceable available security). Assets are valued at 70% of verified valuation and revalued every 180 days; no automatic sale of property. Cash/reserve is segregated; release 90 days after last covered obligation closes if no live claim. A reserve profile withholds 5% of funded clinic principal settlement until cap; clinic payout is gross entitlement less actual reserve withholding, never an undisclosed fee. Cash, collateral and reserve are alternatives unless the contract explicitly sums distinct layers; never double-count coverage.

Customers can dispute identity, payment, pricing, service or collection conduct. Create support case immediately, acknowledge within 4 business hours, resolve operational cases within 2 business days or show escalated creditor/legal owner. A dispute alone does not cancel principal or change due dates; disputed penalty accrual is suspended. Confirmed misposting reverses journal and allocations with evidence. Creditor may grant a documented hardship plan through a signed amendment; baseline prototype records request and approve/reject, with approved plan moving unpaid installments by 30 days once, no added commission, check replacement before activation and old schedule retained as superseded.

## 8. Catalog and five role workspaces

Public `/showcase` searches active clinic services by category, price and clinic; `/b/{slug}` displays inviter-specific branding and eligible services. Each entry shows title, image, price, inventory/appointment availability, clinic, last update and HTTPS clinic link. Catalog data older than 24 hours is hidden from buyable results. Revalidate price and availability synchronously at checkout; stale/changed offer requires customer reconfirmation. Logged-out users see a labeled generic example; only approved eligible customers see a personalized installment quote. No cart or clinical scheduling is owned by PayStar.

Baseline connector is a documented REST/JavaScript integration for clinic websites, plus a WooCommerce adapter using the same order/webhook contracts. Hosted clinic order entry supports clinics without a website and creates the same authoritative order API record. Dedicated showcase includes the inviting clinic's services by default; business-admin can set logo, accent color and order of its own items, but cannot add another clinic's inventory without that clinic's recorded permission. Referral click and purchase share a correlation ID for attribution. Catalog integration failure does not overwrite last known price as zero.

Five canonical roles are mandatory. Permission subscopes within a role (reviewer, treasury, legal, collector, auditor) enforce separation without creating a sixth role. A user may have separate memberships, but may not approve their own initiation even through another membership. `super-admin`: organizations, user access, policy release and global operational health; `paystar-admin`: KYC/review, support, servicing, treasury proposals and reports; `business-admin`: own clinic referrals, orders, service delivery, returns and model-specific approval; `customer`: own identity, applications, purchases, repayments, checks, wallet and disputes; `bank`: own funding/ownership/approval/collections scope, model-specific. No role can edit posted ledger rows.

Contribution per purchase = PayStar earned service/commission revenue minus refunds of that revenue minus variable operating cost minus allocated funding cost minus PayStar contractual loss. Principal collections and bank-owned commission are not PayStar revenue. Assignment proceeds are asset monetization, not automatically revenue; recourse can affect accounting treatment. Record economic ownership separately from statutory derecognition, requiring finance approval. Do not count bank funding cost twice as both facility cost and commission allocation. Model 2 subscription is period revenue, not a fictitious loan spread. Model 4 bank service invoice is separate from customer debt.

Daily liquidity forecast uses opening unrestricted cash + confirmed inflows - clinic payouts - customer refund obligations - bank obligations - operational outflows. Use 30-day daily buckets; promised receivables purchases are excluded until confirmed. Segregated wallet, bank, collateral and reserve cash is unavailable to operating treasury. Maintain projected lowest balance >=0 and minimum 20% buffer over next 7-day committed outflows for new originations. Suspend new originations if breached; repayments/refunds remain operational.

Pilot targets (chosen assumptions): 95% of complete PayStar reviews within 2 business days; 95% clinic settlements within model target; 99.5% monthly app availability; 100% daily bank/ledger reconciliation or quarantined explicit exception; duplicate financial effects=0; 30+ DPD principal / matured eligible cohort principal <=5%; realized fraud loss / originated principal <=1%; positive contribution per purchase after actual costs and losses before expansion. An approval-to-first-purchase conversion target of 40% is a hypothesis, not a promised outcome. Assess repayment only for matured installments and publish cohort vintages; immature accounts do not improve measured default performance. Expansion requires 3 completed monthly cohorts, finance/risk sign-off, zero unresolved high-severity reconciliation defects and partner capacity.

## 10. Acceptance and launch controls

Review is a required deployment gate, not an unanswered product-design question. Unsupported or rejected terms disable new production contracts until a new approved version; existing accounts remain serviced under their lawful agreements.

Separate documents must exist for customer credit/service agreement, clinic participation/refund/guarantee agreement, bank facility/purchase/agency agreement as applicable, servicing/collections mandate, privacy/consent, payment settlement mandate and provider data processing. Prototype agreement PDFs can be clearly labeled simulation documents; they must never claim legal enforceability.

All commercial figures and enforcement thresholds here are design assumptions; current legal and banking approval is still mandatory.
## 11. Model 3 — origination, sale, recourse and servicing

**Flow:** PayStar first pays clinic and owns originated customer debt; later bank pays purchase proceeds to PayStar for specifically accepted assets. Customers initially sign with PayStar and issue checks to PayStar. No clinic payout or customer approval depends on a future bank purchase. A bridge treasury pool of 10,000,000,000 IRR is the prototype funding source; capacity is reserved as in Model 1. Bank rejection leaves the original debt and collection responsibility with PayStar.

A sale lot is the entire remaining three-installment purchase after the first installment has cleared. Partial sale within a purchase is disabled in version 1; a portfolio can contain multiple lots and bank can accept/reject lots independently. Eligibility: confirmed clinic payout and service-start acknowledgment, no return/dispute/overdue balance, verified transferable contract and checks, no lien or existing sale lock, at least 7 calendar days to next maturity and at most 120 days to final maturity. Sell only remaining face after payments/adjustments, never the first installment already collected.

Bank offer identifies lot IDs and hash, purchase date, exact remaining principal and commission, price, discount, fees, recourse mode, guarantee rights, expiry and serving mandate. Prototype price: F=remaining principal+commission; discount=round(F*300/10000), fee=0, holdback=0, cash price=F-discount. For the 40 million toman example after first payment F=336,000,000 IRR, discount=10,080,000, net cash=325,920,000. This is a flat transaction discount, not a market rate or annual yield. Bank may make a larger discount/holdback proposal; PayStar treasury must approve exact economics within its versioned minimum price of 90% of remaining face. Poor offers can be rejected, leaving PayStar funded exposure.

**Transfer protocol:** DRAFT → SUBMITTED → BANK_REVIEW → OFFERED/REJECTED → SELLER_ACCEPTED → TRANSFER_PENDING → TRANSFERRED_PENDING_CASH → SETTLED. Every accepted lot is locked against another sale, return mutation and check presentation. Customer online payment intents are temporarily paused for at most 1 business day; already-in-flight intents must settle or definitively fail before snapshot is frozen. A pending customer return/dispute cancels eligibility rather than trapping the customer. No transfer completes during unresolved payment activity.

Legal effective ownership changes only when executed assignment, bank acceptance, verified required Sayad/check-holder transfer and guarantee-beneficiary evidence are all present. Persist `ownership_effective_at` separately from bank purchase cash. If legal transfer is effective before cash, bank is already owner and PayStar records purchase-price receivable from bank; display `TRANSFERRED_PENDING_CASH`, never incorrectly retain PayStar as owner. Bank must pay by next business day; overdue purchase price becomes bank counterparty exposure and freezes further sales. If cash arrives before transfer, hold it as bank funds payable/suspense and do not derecognize debt or reroute checks. Compensating legal transfer back requires actual reverse-assignment and check evidence, not a database rollback.

At transfer, retain customer installment IDs and amounts, append owner history and notify customer of new creditor, check holder, payment destination and unchanged dates. PayStar remains servicing agent by default; payment interface now routes directly to bank account. A bank-serviced profile is also supported: bank handles payment and sends signed events, PayStar shows read-only balance/status. Mandate expiry stops PayStar collection actions, not the debt. Money mistakenly received in old PayStar account after effective transfer is bank payable and remitted next business day, allocated only once by provider receipt identity. Collections, refunds and recourse always use event-effective owner, not whichever account a callback names.

**With recourse (default fixture):** bank retains customer receivable and may demand repurchase at DPD30 after five calendar days' written cure notice. Demand amount=remaining covered unpaid principal+scheduled unpaid commission minus all intervening recoveries, with no unapproved fee or penalty. PayStar must fund within five business days of valid demand. Repurchase payment alone does not transfer holder rights: bank→PayStar assignment and verified check transfer must complete; until then bank remains creditor and a due-from-bank/right-to-transfer balance is tracked. Recalculate amount at funding to avoid overpaying after customer cure. Final repurchase shifts owner to PayStar and closes bank recourse claim; no new customer debt is created.

**Without recourse:** bank assumes customer insolvency/default after effective transfer. Customer delinquency cannot create a PayStar repurchase demand. Proven fraud by PayStar, duplicate assignment, invalid underlying contract, misrepresentation or breached warranties remain separate indemnity events; they require evidence, legal approval and a specific contractual remedy. Price may be lower, credit filters stricter, only some applicants accepted, and bank may request separately capped guarantees/reserves. A guarantee supported non-recourse sale must disclose who actually retains loss; never market it as complete risk transfer if PayStar guarantees ordinary default. Both modes are immutable per lot and demonstrated in separate fixtures.

Clinic guarantees travel only if assignability and bank beneficiary are documented from inception; otherwise lot is ineligible when bank requires that guarantee. Retain original cap, previously used amount, priority and enforcement trigger across transfer; assignment does not reset coverage. Clinic remains liable for accepted service returns. Post-sale accepted return cancels the customer's eligible debt immediately; PayStar refund bridge pays paid amounts and PayStar compensates bank for cancelled bank-owned remaining face at par as a contractual service-dispute indemnity, recovering principal from clinic and commission from its economic recipient. Bank reverses/refunds commission it received or held; no customer repurchase liability is fabricated. A returned asset is not automatically resold back; close bank-owned receivable by approved cancellation and partner settlement. Track indemnity, commission clawback and discounted purchase economics separately to avoid reimbursing the same cancelled face twice.

Benefits retained: faster conversion of receivables to cash, increased origination capacity and continuing customer relationship; bank buys evaluated originated assets. Risks retained: complex assignment/holder chain, rejected assets, unattractive price, retained risk under recourse, transfer timing mismatch and legal/financial derecognition requirements. Sold lots do not free customer credit. Statutory sale accounting is a finance-approved policy separate from legal owner change.

Model acceptance: bank cannot buy unoriginated debt; duplicate lot sale fails; purchase cash alone cannot change legal owner; legal transfer without cash changes owner and opens bank payable claim; non-recourse ordinary default cannot trigger recourse; recourse/cancellation during receipt processing is serialized and amounts revalued; both bank-serviced and PayStar-serviced routes reconcile.
## Four-model comparison and selection context

| Dimension | Model 1 | Model 2 | Model 3 | Model 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer creditor | PayStar | Selling clinic | PayStar, then bank for transferred assets | Bank from agreement execution |
| Check beneficiary | PayStar | Selling clinic | PayStar, then verified bank holder | Bank from issuance |
| Clinic cash | PayStar pays upfront | Customer pays gradually | PayStar pays upfront before sale | Bank pays upfront |
| Bank relationship | PayStar facility creditor | Usually none | Receivables purchaser | Customer lender |
| PayStar function | Lender and servicer | Facilitator and servicer | Originator, seller and servicer | Facilitator and delegated servicer |
| Default exposure | PayStar | Clinic | Before sale PayStar; afterwards depends on recourse | Bank plus explicit clinic guarantee |
| PayStar capital | High | Low; operational/refund liquidity still required | Temporary origination capital plus retained/recourse risk | Low; operational/refund liquidity still required |
| Transfer complexity | None | None | High | None |
| Clinic payment speed | Fast, subject to confirmed funds | Slow | Fast, independent of sale acceptance | Fast after bank completion |
| Product control | High | Moderate; clinic approves | High origination control; bank eligibility constraints | Bank policy dependent |

Choose Model 1 when product and customer control justify funding and default exposure. Model 2 suits a smaller, faster pilot if clinics accept deferred cash and credit losses; lower capital needs do not guarantee higher margins. Model 3 enables recycling origination liquidity but purchase rejection, discount, recourse and check transfer can limit the benefit. Model 4 gives a clear creditor and beneficiary from inception and avoids subsequent assignment, while bank processing speed and policy constrain the experience. These are four supported prototypes, not an instruction to select just one.
